# SEO Page Title Expansion

Status: partially deferred.

Reminder trigger:
- Open this when working on PDP metadata, search-preview UX, browser title generation, or storefront SEO output.

Current scope:
- Product Editor may show a search preview and editable Meta Description.
- Full SEO page title editing is not part of the current Product Editor pass unless the owner separately approves it.

Future goal:
- Generate SEO page title automatically from product name, key category/spec attributes, and buyer-facing variant context.
- Use it in PDP metadata/browser title.
- Consider editable override only if the UI remains simple.

Guardrails:
- Do not confuse SEO page title with the buyer-visible product name.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Keep title generated from trusted product/category/spec data.
- Seller override should not affect supplier identity or fulfillment.

Potential acceptance checks:
- PDP title is clean and buyer-readable.
- Search preview reflects the generated title and meta description.
- Title remains stable unless product naming/category context changes or seller override is explicitly saved.
